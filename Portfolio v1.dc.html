Mate Store

The Problem

02

Architecture Landing Page

Portfolio site for an architect
Live demo ↗ Code ↗
React TypeScript Tailwind CSS Responsive gallery

The Problem

I built this for my mom, who is an architect and was just starting to move her work online. She wanted a website to show her projects, explain how she works, and make it easy for people to reach her.

What I Did

We worked on the content and structure together. She picked the colors and I suggested the sections that would show her work best. I focused a lot on the project images because I wanted people to see real work right away and trust her experience. The part I'm most proud of is the My Projects section: it shows projects with images of different sizes and their locations, so you can quickly see the kind of work she does. The hardest part was making that gallery look good on smaller screens.

Outcome

This time I planned much better. Before writing any code, I made a prototype in Claude Design and showed it to her first, so we fixed everything in the design. Because of that, almost nothing changed once I started coding and the whole thing went smoothly. It taught me how much good planning saves you later.

03

Garage Manage

Full-stack vehicle maintenance app
Live demo ↗ Code ↗
React Node.js PostgreSQL Auth & sessions Email reminders

The Problem

My dad wanted an easier way to keep track of our family's vehicles. It was always hard to remember each car's maintenance history, upcoming services, and expiration dates.

What I Did

I built an app where you can register your vehicles, keep a maintenance history with dates, costs, and locations, manage expiration dates, and see a dashboard with upcoming reminders. I also added email reminders so people don't forget important dates, and the option to upload PDFs or photos of documents like insurance or inspections. The hardest part was keeping users logged in, at first they lost their data after a while, so I had to really understand how authentication and sessions work to keep each user's information isolated and safe. The features I'm most proud of are the email reminders and the service and expiration sections, because they solve the main problem the app was built for.

Outcome

This project taught me how to integrate a full backend into a real app instead of only building the frontend, and I learned a lot about authentication and user management.
